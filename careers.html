Entry-Level ICT Careers

These are realistic starting points for beginners who complete basic digital skills training. Most of these roles can begin with short courses and a small portfolio.

Entry-Level Role Cards

Explore beginner pathways that require no expensive degrees to get started in Makindye.

Computer Operator / Data Entry

Typing, filing and entering data accurately. Good starting role for office work.

IT Support Assistant

Helping users with basic computer problems, printers and simple network issues.

Digital Content Assistant

Creating simple social media posts, editing short videos or managing basic websites.

Online Customer Support

Answering customer questions by email, chat or phone for companies that sell online.

Skills-to-Role Map

Skill you learn Useful for these roles
Typing and documents Data entry, office assistant, customer support
Internet and email All ICT roles, online support, research
Basic HTML / web Content assistant, junior web helper
Online safety awareness Any role that uses the internet

Beginner Portfolio Ideas

  • A short “About Me” page you built with HTML
  • A one-page CV saved as PDF
  • Screenshots of practice documents you created
  • A simple poster or social media graphic (even if made in Canva or PowerPoint)
  • A short reflection on what you learned in the three-day starter plan

You do not need many items. Three or four clear examples are enough for a beginner portfolio.

CV and Interview Basics

CV tips

  • Keep it to one page if possible
  • Put your name, phone and email at the top
  • List education and any short courses
  • Include skills (typing, MS Word, internet, basic HTML)
  • Add any volunteer or practice projects

Interview tips

  • Arrive on time (or join the call early)
  • Dress neatly
  • Prepare one short story about a problem you solved while learning
  • Ask one thoughtful question at the end
  • Thank the interviewer
