About Kabalagala Digital Pathways

Purpose and Audience

Kabalagala Digital Pathways is a student project created by Group 16 at Victoria University. The website helps young people in Makindye Division (especially around Kabalagala) find clear information about beginner ICT training.

It is designed for school leavers, job seekers and ICT beginners aged roughly 15–30, as well as teachers and mentors who guide them.

The site only provides information. It does not process applications or collect personal data.

Sources and Update Policy

We only list training providers after confirming details on official pages. Each provider card shows a “last checked” date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this website run by KCCA or MTN?

No. This is a student project from Victoria University. We only share publicly available information.

Can I apply for training through this website?

No. All applications must be made on the provider’s official website or at the training centre.

Do you collect my personal information?

We only collect the contact information you voluntarily submit in our inquiry form to reply to your questions. Your details are never sold, tracked, or used for commercial marketing.

How can I suggest a new training provider?

You can use the inquiry form above, email info@kabalagaladigitalpathways.ac.ug, or contact Group 16 through your lecturer or class representative. We only add providers after official verification.

Privacy Notice

Privacy Commitment: We only collect the contact information you voluntarily submit in our inquiry form to reply to your questions. Your details are never sold, tracked, or used for commercial marketing.

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising trackers. When you reach out via our contact inquiry form or email, your details are retained solely to respond to your inquiry and are never shared with third parties.

External links (for example to KCCA or news sites) are provided for information only. Those sites have their own independent privacy policies.

If you have questions about this project or your data, email info@kabalagaladigitalpathways.ac.ug or speak to a member of Group 16.
