    Online Safety & Cyber Hygiene

    Essential habits to safeguard your phone, computer accounts, and personal money while searching for training or job opportunities online.

    Security Habit 01

    1. How to Vet a Website

    • Always check the domain extension: look for .go.ug (government) and .ac.ug (academic institutions).
    • Look for the padlock symbol in the browser address bar (HTTPS encryption).
    • Be cautious of sites demanding registration money before providing course details.
    • Search the organization name + "scam" or "reviews" before submitting personal documents.
    Security Habit 02

    2. Strong Passwords & Two-Step Login (2FA)

    • Use passwords with at least 8–12 characters mixing uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
    • Never reuse the same password across multiple email and banking accounts.
    • Enable Two-Step Verification (2FA) on your Gmail, WhatsApp, and social accounts.
    • Never disclose verification codes sent via SMS to anyone who calls or texts you.

    Common Recruitment Red Flags in Kabalagala & Kampala:

    • Any recruiter asking for "interview fees", "uniform fees", or "medical examination deposits" via mobile money.
    • Urgent pressure: "Send money within 30 minutes to secure your slot."
    • Job offers sent through unofficial personal Gmail accounts claiming to represent reputable national agencies.

    Faculty of Science & Technology · Victoria University Kampala

    Kabalagala Digital Pathways was conceptualized and engineered under Group 16 at Victoria University Kampala. The project addresses the critical urban digital divide documented by the African Cities Research Consortium (ACRC, 2026) and UBOS, which revealed that while youth in informal settlements have high mobile phone access, fewer than 18% possess formal digital skills required for office and data processing employment.
